## 5. Measurable international conversion goals
Set targets after a **4-week baseline** (the dashboard gives each metric below; do not copy numbers from other companies). Suggested form: baseline + a stated improvement, reviewed monthly.

| Goal | Definition (source) |
|---|---|
| International inquiry volume | Inquiries per month with a country other than your own (International tab; filter by country) |
| Qualified inquiry rate | Inquiries with a service, budget and timeline filled in ÷ all inquiries (Admin inbox) |
| Quotation request share | "Customised quotation" ÷ all inquiries (International tab) |
| Meeting request rate | Meeting requests ÷ inquiries + meeting requests (International tab) |
| Meeting confirmation rate | Confirmed ÷ requested (Admin → Meetings) |
| First-reply time | Median hours and % within 24 h (International tab, computed from your replies) |
| Country reach | Distinct countries with inquiries per month (International tab) |
| Content-to-inquiry | Service/blog/portfolio pages viewed by consenting visitors that precede an inquiry (Analytics → Contacts) |
| Inquiry to proposal | Quotations sent ÷ inquiries (track manually in the inbox until volume justifies a field) |
| Proposal to project | Accepted quotations ÷ quotations sent (manual) |

Notes: visit-based figures include only visitors who accepted statistics and are approximate; form-based figures include everyone who wrote to you.
