# Methods

* **Classification**: image resized (short side 256) and centre-cropped to 224, scaled to [−1, 1], MobileNetV2 → softmax over 1000 ImageNet classes.
* **Detection**: YOLOX-S at 640×640 letterbox, score = objectness × class probability, class-wise NMS (0.45). Video: every n-th frame to reach the chosen rate (default 3 fps), boxes drawn, H.264 MP4 written. No tracking, so counts are detections per frame.
* **Segmentation**: DeepLabV3-MobileNetV2 at 257×257; logits resized to the image, argmax per pixel over 21 VOC classes; the PNG mask is RGBA (background transparent).
* **OCR**: pdfium text layer when ≥ 20 characters, otherwise RapidOCR (PP-OCR det/cls/rec); fields from regular expressions for dates, e-mail, currency amounts, `+` phone numbers and reference numbers.
* **Forecasting**: dates parsed and the series regularised to a detected frequency (minute…year; gaps ≤ 20 % interpolated, reported). The last `max(h, 3)` points (≤ n/4) are held out; naive, seasonal naive, damped Holt and damped additive Holt-Winters are fitted on the rest and scored (MAE, RMSE, MAPE). *Auto* refits the lowest-MAE method on everything. Interval = ±1.96 × the hold-out error standard deviation (approximate; omitted under 5 hold-out points).
* **Sensors**: *rolling z-score* uses the median and MAD of the preceding window (adapts to, and then misses, sustained faults and flat lines); *baseline z-score* uses the first share of the data (default 25 %) as the healthy reference; *Isolation Forest* (200 trees, seed 0) on standardised readings plus rolling mean and standard deviation. Events merge flagged points separated by small gaps. With a label column, precision, recall, F1 and labelled events found are computed. On the bundled synthetic pump data at window 48, z 5, contamination 0.03 the measured scores are: baseline precision 1.00 / recall 0.81 (3 of 5 events), rolling z 0.36 / 0.06, Isolation Forest 0.49 / 0.10 — no method finds the stuck sensor, which is a flat line at a normal value.
* **Pose**: YOLOX person boxes (score ≥ 0.35, up to 8 per image, ≥ 40 px tall) → BlazePose on a square crop → 33 keypoints with visibility; joint angles are computed only where the three joints have visibility ≥ 0.5.
* **Search**: passages of about 70 words ending at sentence boundaries with a 15-word overlap; tokens without stop words; **semantic** = cosine of IDF-weighted mean word vectors (300-d spaCy `en_core_web_md`, 20 000 vectors, words without a vector skipped and listed); **keyword** = BM25 (k1 1.2, b 0.75); **hybrid** = α·minmax(semantic) + (1−α)·minmax(BM25).
* **Recommendation**: MobileNetV2 1280-d pooled features of each product image (28×28 upscaled to 224), cosine; TF-IDF (1–2 grams, sublinear) of name, category, brand, colour, material and description, cosine; score = w·image + (1−w)·text. Explanations list shared attributes and the words contributing most to the text similarity.
