# Methods

* **PDF text layer** (`embedded.py`): pypdfium2 returns every character with a loose box. Characters are clustered into lines by vertical position and split into items at gaps wider than the text height, or wider than 0.3× with no space character in between (separately placed table cells have none). Exact per-character x positions are kept so field boxes are exact. Rotated pages are read with OCR instead.
* **Skew** (`ocr.py`): the page is binarised and rotated over −8…+8°; the angle that maximises the variance of the row-sum profile (coarse 0.5° then fine 0.1°) is the correction, applied only if ≥ 0.3° and the profile sharpens by ≥ 5 %. On the simulated scan (skewed 1.4°) it finds −1.4°.
* **OCR** (`ocr.py`): RapidOCR runs PP-OCR v3 detection, a 180° line classifier and recognition. *High* sensitivity lowers the detector and score thresholds (0.3 / 0.3, unclip 2.0 instead of 0.5 / 0.5 / 1.6): on the simulated scan this recovered the single-digit quantity cells and spaces the defaults missed (20 of 20 cells exact instead of 17); on the clean samples it made no difference. It can add noise on messy pages, which the confidence and the red boxes show.
* **Tables** (`layout.py`): adaptive threshold, morphological opening with long horizontal and vertical kernels, connected line structures, row and column edges from where strokes lie (≥ 2 rows and ≥ 2 columns), text assigned to cells by centre. Tables without ruling lines are not found.
* **Headings**: items at least 1.35× the median text height, ≤ 90 characters, outside tables.
* **Fields** (`extract.py`): `label: value` pairs (label ≤ 5 words, not a clock time or URL); a known-label list for label and value in separate items (Total, Subtotal, VAT, Amount due, Invoice Date, Due Date…); stand-alone dates, e-mail addresses, currency amounts and `+` phone numbers inside text. Dates: ISO, `14 March 2024`, `March 14, 2024`, numeric with day > 12 settled, numeric with both ≤ 12 **kept raw and flagged ambiguous** with both readings; spaces are optional between parts because OCR drops them. Amounts need a currency symbol/code or a money-like label; the currency is inferred from the symbol and says so. Every field stores the rule that produced it.
* **Checks**: subtotal + tax = total; quantity × unit price = amount per table row; sum of table amounts = subtotal. Run only when the values exist; a failure flags the fields involved.
* **Classification**: keyword vote over `invoice, receipt, letter, report, terms_and_conditions`; a label needs ≥ 3 distinct keywords and a lead of 2, otherwise *unclassified*. Matched keywords are shown.
* **Searchable PDF** (`exportpdf.py`): the page image plus each line as invisible text (render mode 3), scaled horizontally to the line box. Standard Helvetica only: characters outside Windows-1252 become `?` and are counted.
